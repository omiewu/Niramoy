# Niramoy Care Commerce

## Point of view

Niramoy is a care desk rather than a generic product shelf. It makes someone feel oriented before they buy: what they need, how it will arrive, and what to do next. The visual tone is quietly premium, practical, and locally familiar.

## Foundation

Deep blue-green ink is the emotional anchor and jade is the primary action color. The canvas is a barely warm off-white; cards are white with fine mineral-green outlines. Product offers may use a small blue discount ticket and ratings use gold. Soft shadows and gentle tints are used sparingly to give action cards and floating controls a friendly, tactile presence.

Fraunces is reserved for major page statements. DM Sans handles shopping, utility, labels, and body copy.

## Navigation

The header has three layers: an unobtrusive service promise, a main row with the logo and generous search input, then a horizontally scrollable category index. On small screens, search remains immediately available below the compact brand row.

## Home composition

The homepage begins with a full-width carousel. Immediately below it is the `Care, made easy` action grid: WhatsApp ordering, prescription upload, offers, care guidance, live support, and lab-test discovery. The grid uses six cards on desktop, three at tablet sizes, and two on narrow phones.

Product discovery follows through horizontally scrollable shelves: Best Picks, Beauty & Personal Care, Health & Wellness, and Baby & Mom Care. A quick-access category grid, trust indicators, the Niramoy promise, payment and delivery panels, and a comprehensive footer complete the page.

## Product cards

Product cards use a calm white image stage, compact blue discount ticket, two-line product title, gold 4.5-star rating with a review count, current price, crossed-out previous price, and a small outlined ADD control aligned at the lower right. Do not add descriptive subtitles beneath titles; ratings carry the supporting context.

## Action and support controls

The fixed lower-right control stack contains an always-visible jade chat control that opens WhatsApp support. A white back-to-top control appears only after the visitor has scrolled beyond the first viewport and returns smoothly to the page top. Both controls use Lucide icons, generous hit areas, and a slight lift on hover.

## Footer and service panels

Payment methods and delivery partners are displayed as supplied image assets in framed white panels. The footer uses the ink surface, grouped links, app-download badges, and local social-logo assets. Keep social links concise and legible.

## Component rules

- Primary buttons: jade fill, white semibold text, 8-10px radius.
- Secondary/action-card buttons: white surface with clear accent-colored text.
- Product controls: compact but high-contrast; the ADD button must remain aligned with prices.
- Inputs: 44px minimum height, 10px radius, crisp jade focus ring.
- Icons: use Lucide for interface actions and category illustration; use brand assets only where a recognizable social mark is required.

## Responsive behavior

At tablet widths, product shelves show roughly two cards at a time and care actions reduce to three columns. At narrow mobile widths, care actions become a two-column grid, product rails remain swipeable, navigation stays scrollable, and fixed controls sit 15px from the viewport edges. Touch targets should remain at least 43-44px.

## Guardrails

- Do not imply medical efficacy, diagnosis, or dosage instructions.
- Do use direct labels, Bangladesh-appropriate delivery and payment language, and a strong visible hierarchy.
- Keep promotional color as a supporting accent; the jade-and-ink brand system remains dominant.
